Basic Challenge Information

Need more help? Check out the Getting Started page!

  • This version contains mostly one Pokédex entry per evolution line on Global PokédeX Plus with the exception of a few.
  • Alternate Arceus forms will not be counted, due to the harsh method of obtaining the species.
  • Deerling icons will be used over Sawsbuck due to Deerling being unable to form change while Sawsbuck can. This means you can hatch any one Deerling form and obtain all Sawsbuck, but still be missing the other three Deerling.
  • If a Pokémon has a Novelty form that can be reversed, its official form will be used over its Novelty counterpart.
  • If a Pokémon has a Novelty form that can't be reversed, both its official form and its Novelty counterpart are used.
  • The official National Pokédex order takes priority in the sorting of entries, including Novelty Pokémon.
  • Pokémon with alternate forms such as Mega Evolutions, Gigantamax forms, and Regional variants are not included in the generation their form was introduced in. Instead, they are grouped with the original Pokémon they are based on, as they share the same Pokédex number in the official series. This does not apply to Convergent species, as each has its own separate entry. These Pokémon will also use their official form name over what's displayed on Global PokédeX Plus.
  • At times, you may see a 'Beast Ball' icon in place of a Pokémon; it represents placeholder data and will eventually be replaced.
  • Pokédex Requirements Key:
  • ⚠️ Possible to miss.
  • 🔄 Reversible through current form.
  • ⏪ Reversible only through another form.
  • 🧬 Requires DNA Splicers.
  • PCDex+ Features:
  • Customizable filter options by generation, type, rarity, form, gender, color, egg steps, egg groups, and ownership.
  • Cheats that allow you to quickly switch between marking every Pokémon as obtained, which can be useful for viewing purposes, especially during a Poké Hunt, restoring your previous data, or wiping your progress completely.
  • Summary when hovering over Pokémon icons that displays which generation they were introduced in, their Pokédex number, species name, form, gender, rarity, and typing.
  • Multiple ways to import over your shiny collection by either: left-clicking its icon, searching for its name in the "Jump to a Pokémon" menu, or by editing through an HTML editor.

The Cheat Menu (↑↑↓↓←→←→B A Start)

As you may know, each icon displays by default with a light opacity, and when hovered over or clicked, it becomes full opacity. But what if you wanted to temporarily view each icon with full opacity? It could be useful for simple viewing purposes, or perhaps you may consider using this tool for the Poké Hunt, especially since new filter options have been added! These cheats will affect anything added through JavaScript; anything hardcoded in your HTML file will remain untouched!
